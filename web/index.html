03 / SYSTEMS

Every worker counts.
Not always equally.

Two workers see different numbers of examples. Their mean gradients are −4 and −10. The larger shard should carry more of the update.

Worker A keeps two examples. This desk holds local gradients fixed to isolate weighting. The Python lesson computes real gradients from synthetic shards.
